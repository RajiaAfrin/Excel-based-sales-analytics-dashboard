# Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis

## Project Background

Ferns and Petals (FNP) is a leading gifting and e-commerce company that specializes in flowers, cakes, personalized gifts, soft toys, and festive products. Understanding customer purchasing behavior, product performance, seasonal demand, and delivery efficiency is essential for improving sales performance and customer satisfaction.

This Excel-based sales analysis project examines order activity, revenue generation, product performance, customer purchasing trends, and occasion-based demand patterns. The goal is to identify key revenue drivers, understand customer purchasing behavior, and generate actionable business insights.

## Executive Summary

### Overview of Findings

The analysis generated revenue of **₹3,520,984** from **1,000 orders**, with an average customer spend of **₹3,520.98** and an average delivery time of **5.53 days**.

Revenue was highly concentrated around celebration-driven occasions, particularly **Anniversary**, **Raksha Bandhan**, and **Holi**. Certain product categories, including **Colors**, **Soft Toys**, and **Sweets**, generated a significant share of total revenue.

The analysis also revealed clear geographic differences in ordering activity, with cities such as **Imphal**, **Dhanbad**, and **Kavali** generating the highest order volumes.

## Insights Deep Dive

## 1. Sales Performance Overview

The dashboard highlights the following key business metrics:

- **Total Orders:** 1,000
- **Total Revenue:** ₹3,520,984
- **Average Customer Spend:** ₹3,520.98
- **Average Delivery Time:** 5.53 Days

These metrics provide a high-level view of business performance and customer purchasing behavior.

## 2. Revenue by Occasion

Occasion-based purchasing plays a major role in revenue generation.

The highest revenue-generating occasions were:

| Occasion | Revenue |
|-----------|---------:|
| Anniversary | ₹674,634 |
| Raksha Bandhan | ₹631,585 |
| Holi | ₹574,682 |
| Birthday | ₹408,194 |
| Valentine's Day | ₹331,930 |
| Diwali | ₹313,783 |

Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan generated the largest share of revenue, highlighting the importance of seasonal and gifting-related events.

## 3. Product Category Performance

Category analysis shows that revenue is concentrated within a few high-performing product categories.

Top-performing categories include:

- Colors
- Soft Toys
- Sweets

These categories consistently generated strong sales and contributed significantly to total revenue.

## 4. Top Products by Revenue

## 6. Customer Ordering Behavior

Revenue by order hour analysis revealed clear patterns in customer purchasing activity throughout the day.

Ordering activity was concentrated during specific hours, helping identify periods of higher customer engagement and purchasing intent.

Understanding these patterns can support marketing campaign scheduling and promotional planning.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, I would recommend the following:

### 1. Prioritize High-Revenue Occasions

Occasions such as Anniversary, Raksha Bandhan, and Holi generate significant revenue and should receive focused marketing and promotional efforts.

### 2. Expand High-Performing Product Categories

Categories such as Colors, Soft Toys, and Sweets consistently contribute strong sales performance and may benefit from expanded product offerings.

### 3. Focus Marketing Efforts on High-Demand Cities

Cities with high order volumes represent valuable customer markets and should be prioritized for targeted promotions and customer retention initiatives.

### 4. Optimize Product Portfolio

Top-performing products should receive additional visibility and promotional support to maximize revenue potential.

### 5. Improve Delivery Operations

Monitoring delivery performance and maintaining low delivery times can help improve customer satisfaction and repeat purchases.

## Assumptions and Caveats

- Revenue figures are based on the provided order data.
- Customer purchasing behavior may vary over time and across regions.
- The analysis reflects historical transactions included in the dataset.
- Delivery performance is measured using the available delivery date information.
- Results may vary if additional data becomes available.
